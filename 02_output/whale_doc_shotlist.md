## How scenes connect
All 44 joins between shots are camera moves of 20 frames, centered on the frame in the table above. Each shot's `link` in the source sets its type:
- **push:** through a point in the shot and out of a point in the next. For example, into the photo of the lump, through the whale's eye, and from the whale's head into its brain
- **tilt:** down or up across a dark gap, so sinking or rising through the water
- **pan:** sideways across a dark gap
- **blend:** two shots in the same place with the same camera, so they play as one continuous shot

- **Bus:** no length is shown, because the script gives none. It is drawn at about 70% of the whale's width, as in frame 5a.
