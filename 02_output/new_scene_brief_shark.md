- Length (seconds): 5 (150 frames at 30 fps)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9, 1920 x 1080
- Output format: MP4 with sound

## Content
- Characters / objects (original, not copied from the reference): a Greenland shark, a flashlight, a row of person icons
- Script or voiceover text: no voiceover. On-screen text only: "HOW OLD IS THIS SHARK?", "~5 M", "≈ 400 YEARS = 4-5 LIFETIMES"
- Key moments, in order:
  1. Frames 0-62, sonar scene: the shark swims through a flashlight cone, sonar rings expand from it, the title snaps in
  2. Frame 63, hard cut to the scale scene: rule and length label
  3. Red caption, then person icons pop in one at a time, the fifth ghosted to show "4 to 5"

## Fact basis
Nielsen et al., Science, 2016: radiocarbon dating of 28 Greenland sharks. The largest, 502 cm long, was estimated at 392 ± 120 years. "~5 M" and "≈ 400 YEARS" both come from that animal. "4-5 lifetimes" assumes a lifetime of 80 to 100 years.
