# New Scene Brief

The earlier 5-second shark brief is kept in `new_scene_brief_shark.md`.

## What to make
- Topic / story: കടലിന്റെ ഇരുട്ടിലെ ശബ്ദം, the story of the sperm whale, told through the ambergris lump
- Length: 6 to 7 minutes in 10 sections, built in stages
- Built: all 10 sections, 6:45, 12,150 frames at 30 fps
- Aspect ratio: 16:9, 1920 x 1080
- Output format: MP4 with sound

## User decisions
- Build order: storyboard and section 1 first, so the look is approved before the other 6 minutes are built
- Voiceover: none for now. Scenes use the script's estimated timings and must be re-timed when narration is added
- On-screen text: Malayalam titles and captions, with numbers and units in the guide's English style (`~16 M`, `236 dB`, `40 TONNES`)

## Content
- Script: the Malayalam script supplied by the user, section by section in `whale_doc_shotlist.md`
- Characters / objects, all drawn new: sperm whale, ambergris lump, newspaper clippings, squid and more per the shot list
- Clippings carry no real newspaper name or masthead. Their only words are the script's own headline phrases

## Style rules
Follow `01_analysis/style_guide.md` exactly. Anything this brief changes on purpose:
- Titles are Malayalam, so the guide's all-caps rule cannot apply to them. They use Noto Sans Malayalam Black in the same color, size and position. Latin numbers and units stay in Montserrat Black
- The subject is the same species as the reference, so the whale must be redrawn, not traced. It faces left (the reference's faces right) and has its own outline
- Newspaper paper and the ambergris lump have no guide colors. Both are built from the guide's greys and creams and are listed as invented in the changelog
- Elephants are left out: they are the reference's device and this script does not mention them

## Deliverables
- `whale_doc_storyboard.jpg` and `whale_doc_shotlist.md`
- 12 clips in `whale_doc_pieces/`. A full video is made only when the user allows it
- A short changelog note on what matches the reference and what is approximated
